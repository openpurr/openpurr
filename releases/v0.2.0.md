Moving from RevenueCat: import your existing customers, with a step-by-step guide for switching an app that's already live.

## What's new

**Import customers from RevenueCat**
- `openpurr import FILE.csv` brings your existing customers over from RevenueCat's export. openpurr doesn't copy RevenueCat's purchase records: for each row it asks Apple or Google for the purchase, as it does when your app posts one, and records the store's answer for that app user.
- **Reads RevenueCat's files as they are.** Column names are matched loosely, and App Store transaction IDs, Google Play purchase tokens, aliases, `first_seen` and customer attributes (from an Audience export) are all recognized.
- **Safe to run on a live deployment:**
  - it never takes a purchase from a customer who already has it;
  - it sends no webhooks;
  - it never overwrites attributes your app set;
  - it stops at 90% of a free-plan daily limit.
- **Safe to stop and re-run.** `--dry-run` asks the stores but writes nothing. The CLI saves its progress, so a stopped import resumes where it left off; it retries when a store is busy and writes problems to a report.
- **Charts:** imported customers keep their original first-seen date and count in the new-customers chart. Chart history before the switch is partial, since openpurr keeps the latest transaction of each subscription.
- Owners and admins only. From the CLI, use an access token with the **admin** scope.

**Migration guide**
- [Moving from RevenueCat](https://github.com/openpurr/openpurr/blob/main/docs/migrating-from-revenuecat.md) walks through the switch in seven steps: setting up openpurr, keeping both backends informed while older app versions still talk to RevenueCat (Apple notification forwarding, a second Pub/Sub subscription for Google Play), the export to ask RevenueCat for, the import, a one-time `syncPurchases` in your app update, checking, and finishing the switch.
- It also lists what moves and what doesn't.

**Website**
- A [Migrate from RevenueCat](https://openpurr.org/migrate-from-revenuecat/) page, and the home page in German, French and Spanish.

## Upgrading

Lite deploys from `main`, so a deployment that follows `main` already has these changes. No database or app changes are needed.

To import, update the CLI:

```sh
npm install -g @openpurr/cli@0.2.0
```
